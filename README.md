[INSTALL: Download mp_AISurvival.zip from this repository, then extract the folder in the zip called mp_AISurvival to your appdata/local/plutonium/storage/t5/mods, or wherever you put mod folders for the way you play black ops]
This is the version of Survival in Black Ops 1 that my friend and I had been wanting to make for a while. Imagine if Black Ops had its own version of the mw3 survival mode, but with the black ops universe aesthetic. Compatible with all mp maps in Black Ops, this mod seeks to bring the true survival mode feel from mw3 into BO1 with waves of relentless enemies, boss enemies, buy stations, and even some additional added mods imported from BO1 sp/zombies to add that treyarch look to the exact way the mw3 survival plays.

About 13 years ago, someone made a survival mod for black ops mp that was going for this idea. However, as impressive as it was for an early mp mod on black ops, it did not have too many features to it and visually looked more like MP with some tweaks to it, rather than looking like a survival mode. We built upon this old mod's foundation to fully add 4 buy stations around the map used with earned cash, waves that have mixed types of enemies, ability to use (almost) all streaks at buy station, and varying enemy behaviors like throwing things and dropping to prone or even jumping at you.
Visually, elements like enemy names, killfeed and kill related splash screens are removed so that it feels more like an sp/coop type mode. Enemies will slowly approach at first, with more aggressive behavior later on, moving in "squads" at you similar to how enemies came in groups on mw3 survival. Enemy types of weapons used changes with waves, in addition to juggernauts being mixed in later on with regular enemies. You also have to watch out for enemy napalm strikes intermittently and dogs, which can both keep spawning so long as there is 1 enemy alive in the wave. Note - revives and 1 self rez per wave is only after buying second chance perk - otherwise, you just die.
Boss waves are 1-2 enemy cobras, juggernaut only waves with buffed health, and every 15 waves being an enemy gunship/hind which damages you much more than the cobra heli seen every 5 waves. Finally, wave 40 is the last wave, with some hard bosses involved with buffed health of course, granted you can make it that far.

Mod is essentially finished, with small changes to likely come. (NOTE: This mod was developed independently based on my friend and I wanting to improve the OG survival mod for this game for a while now, and we are not associated with anyone else who has developed similar mods for BO1. For another cool take on a surv mod for BO1, check out user @Polacco 's mp survival mod on the plutonium discord server, who has done more or less the same idea but with direct model imports from mw3 and different animations/stations/hud).
